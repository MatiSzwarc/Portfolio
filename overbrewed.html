Overbrewed is a chaotic multiplayer magic shop simulator built in Unreal Engine 5. Players collaborate in real-time to harvest arcane ingredients, brew temperamental potions, fulfill customer orders under strict time limits, and maintain a functional storefront amid magical accidents.

Magic & Systems Architecture

In Overbrewed, magic is divided into 5 distinct elemental categories: Fire, Wind, Earth, Water, and Heal. As System Designer, I developed the recipe combination matrix and thermodynamic brewing mechanics that dictate how cauldron temperatures, ingredient states, and catalyst reagents react.

  • Ingredient Combining & Volatility: Programmed logic where over-stirring or blending incompatible elements causes volatile reactions, bubbling cauldrons, and temporary shop hazards.
  • Cooperative Order Management: Engineered the customer demand curve so players must divide roles between fetching herbs, monitoring fires, bottling, and customer delivery.
  • Niagara Particle Effects: Authored custom Niagara particle systems in Unreal Engine 5 for dynamic cauldron boiling, shimmering arcane vapors, luminous spell bursts, and explosive failure puffs.
  • Economy & Shop Progression: Balanced upgrade costs for expanded prep tables, automated potion chillers, and rare seed incubators.
