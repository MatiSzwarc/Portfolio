Domefall is a mobile tower defense roguelite where each defeat allows players to earn evolution points, unlock permanent upgrades, and refine tactical turret builds to progress further across waves of escalating threats.

Game Balancing & Numerical Economy

During the development process, simple ideas multiplied into a complex matrix of numbers: How many enemies per wave? How many hits does the player tower require to kill an enemy? What type should an enemy be? How much gold should the player have during game? How should difficulty scale over playtime?

These questions were solved through meticulous spreadsheet mathematical modeling and iterative playtest adjustments. The balancing framework was constructed modularly to allow simple level expansion and the introduction of three distinct difficulty modes.

System Design, Game Loop & Dual Progression

The game architecture revolves around a central player-controlled core dome and auxiliary defensive turrets operated by AI. The progression structure features a dual economy: temporary in-run tactical upgrades purchased with in-game gold, and permanent metaprogression evolution points awarded upon death.

Every tower archetype possesses a branching skill tree with transformative path choices that radically shift projectile properties, damage types, and behavioral AI. Players can also reallocate points if a specific boss wave demands a tactical pivot.

Level Design & Spawner Flow

Level design in Domefall is inextricably linked to balance. I positioned spawner nodes across different map edges and designed the path curves towards the central objective. By tuning spawner quantities, enemy velocities, and staggered wave compositions across each lane, I created levels with distinct tactical priorities and pacing.
