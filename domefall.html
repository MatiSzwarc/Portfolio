Balancing

Domefall is a roguelite experience. I was responsible for creating and maintaining a balancing document in Excel, covering at least 20 levels. I ensured that each level featured different enemy types, varied enemy counts, and spawns from different directions. The goal was to create a progression curve where players would eventually need to die and purchase permanent upgrades to overcome increasingly difficult levels.

Level Designer

Using the balancing document as a reference, I decided where to position enemy spawners around the map and created the initial level blockouts. Once the this were ready, I handed them over to the artists to replace the placeholders.

VFX Art

I created visual effects for projectile firing, enemy deaths, and several other gameplay events.
