Overview & Roles

Product Owner Lead Designer VFX Artist AI Programmer Level Design

Catsmith is a chaotic local co-op smithing simulator built in Unreal Engine 5. Players team up to manage a bustling cat-run forge, coordinate order queues, master distinct mini-games for weapon forging, and satisfy demanding feline customers under time limits.

  • Product Ownership & Design Leadership: Aligned an 11-person team across gameplay, animation, and art to deliver milestone deliverables on time.
  • Cooperative Gameplay Loop: Balanced fast-paced station mechanics where players must communicate to fetch ingots, operate bellows, and assemble finished blades.
  • Dual StateTree AI: Authored distinct Unreal Engine StateTree behaviors for arriving feline Customers and the demanding Shop Master.
  • VFX & Visual Juice: Authored tactile Niagara spark bursts, heat glows, and order alert animations to make forging actions feel responsive and punchy.

Gameplay Systems & Forge Progression (01 – 03)

The core gameplay loop is structured around three key phases: order ingestion, synchronous crafting minigames, and final delivery under escalating time pressure. (Click any image to inspect in full resolution):

Phase 01 Customer Arrival & Order Management

Customers approach the counter driven by StateTree AI with randomized weapon commissions, patience meters, and payment tiers.

Phase 02 Cooperative Forging & Station Minigames

Distinct workstation mini-games: heating ingots, rhythmic hammer strikes, quench baths, and sharpening wheels requiring tight cooperation.

Phase 03 Assembly, Delivery & Rating System

Final inspection by the Forge Master AI, earning gold and reputation points to upgrade workshop stations and unlock new blade recipes.

Systems & Technical Implementation

  • Local Multiplayer Architecture: Seamless couch co-op support with gamepad focus management and shared screen boundary constraints.
  • Minigame Variety: Each station features unique tactile input timing, preventing repetitive player actions and encouraging constant role rotation.
  • Responsive VFX Feedback: Niagara bursts for successful hits, glowing thermal textures on cooling metal, and humorous fail effects when ingots overheat.
  • Dynamic AI State Trees: StateTree evaluation ensuring customers queue orderly, react to wait times, and celebrate successful deliveries.
