My Contribution

Product Owner Lead Designer Level Designer AI Programmer VFX Artist

Product Owner

I was responsible for coordinating the team and managing the development process. This included daily meetings, discussing deadlines, identifying potential problems, and reviewing our progress.

I helped the team establish priorities by distinguishing between essential features and nice-to-have additions, ensuring we stayed focused on delivering the game within our time constraints.

Lead Designer

As both Product Owner and Lead Designer, I was responsible for making final design decisions during brainstorming sessions and guiding the team towards a cohesive gameplay experience.

My work involved extensive discussions about the game's crafting systems, workstations, and overall gameplay loop. I helped shape these elements to ensure they worked together to create an engaging and consistent experience.

Level Designer

The main shop area evolved several times to better support the gameplay loop. I designed the paths and layout to guide players naturally through the crafting process, from receiving an initial recipe to delivering the finished product.

I ultimately settled on a circular layout with intersecting paths in the middle. This structure helped players navigate the shop intuitively while occasionally breaking their usual routes, adding variety to their movement through the space.

AI Programmer

The game features two distinct NPC types: the Master and the Customers.

The Master moves around the shop, occasionally obstructing the player's path. He comments on the quality of crafted items and provides helpful tips to guide players through the game.

The Customers follow a more complex behaviour system. They enter the shop, check whether the counter is available, and wait until they can place an order or lose patience. The system also allows up to three customers to wait for their orders to be completed simultaneously.

VFX Artist

Due to time constraints, we focused on a small number of visual effects that would have the greatest impact on the gameplay experience.

I primarily worked on effects that communicated player actions, including a circular effect around the player, dust clouds during movement, and effects for hammering and crafting.

I also programmed the forge's fire effects, adjusting their parameters dynamically during gameplay to communicate the forge's current state. This allowed players to easily identify whether the forge was lit and ready to use.
