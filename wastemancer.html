Narrative Designer

Wastemancer takes place in a world where animals behave like humans. The main character is a possum working in a magical recycling department.

As the city's defenders capable of fighting trash monsters with magic, you must defeat these creatures and recycle their souls before they overrun the city!

Level Designer

I was responsible for designing the main environment and establishing the camera perspective.

I started by sketching the initial layout on paper, then translated it into a digital version and created a blockout for the artists to develop further.

Throughout the process, I collaborated with the artists to discuss the scene's composition and identify additional elements that needed to be included.

VFX Artist

I created five different skills from scratch using Unity's Visual Effect Graph: Water, Earth, Fire, Wind, and Healing.

I designed this skills around single-target and multi-target mechanics, ensuring that each effect supported the intended gameplay functionality.
