Overview & Roles

Narrative Design Level Design VFX Art

Wastemancer is an arcade action game designed to be easy to learn but hard to master, letting players jump straight into satisfying magical combat against relentless hordes of trash-monsters.

  • VFX Art: Created custom textures and meshes in Photoshop and Blender, built shaders, and programmed dynamic particle behaviors in Unity VFX Graph.
  • 5 Magic Types: Designed Fire, Wind, Earth, Water, and Heal with reactive feedback and positioning-dependent mechanics.
  • Level Design: Crafted combat arena from paper concept through graybox blockout, lighting, camera setup, and post-processing.
  • Narrative Worldbuilding: Defined character lore, world consistency, and why enemies manifest as animated scrap.

Level Design & Arena Graybox (Lvl 01 – 04)

Due to the project's rapid one-month development timeframe, we focused on crafting a single polished combat arena. I drafted the initial layout on paper, blocked out geometry in 3D, and closely collaborated with artists to populate props and sightlines. Once the blockout was validated in playtests, I completed the scene setup with dynamic lighting, camera angle tuning, and post-processing volumes. (Click any image to inspect in full resolution):

Narrative Design

The narrative role in Wastemancer was crucial to bridge disparate gameplay concepts into a cohesive thematic whole. I anchored the explanation of why characters cast magic in a wasteland, why environmental debris comes to life as aggressive monsters, and who our protagonist is. This narrative clarity energized our art team and ensured every visual element reinforced our world logic.
