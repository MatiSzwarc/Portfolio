Narrative Design

We decided to build the game's narrative around a combination of cute aesthetics and dark themes, ultimately creating a necromancer cat as our main character.

We integrated the narrative into the gameplay through events that show up as players progress. Each event presents three different choices, leading to different outcomes depending on the player's decisions.

Quest Design

The game features around 17 unique story events, categorized into Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels based on the player's progression.

Each event was designed to tell a different story within the game's world, introducing situations that could occur during gameplay, the characters involved, and the possible outcomes of the player's choices.

System Design

At the end of the project I decided to go through it and make game playable, so current state of the game took me around month of looking at someone else code, making it work better and as it should.

I fixed game ending after few days, fixed npcs walking around to go inside building at night and small other bugs made during harsh time of week development. I learned from that a lot about coding in Unity from more experienced people than me.

VFX Art

The game features several particle effects, with a particular focus on the base energy resource called PURR.

While experimenting with ways to animate this energy, I ended up creating a shader. It was the first advanced shader I had made for my portfolio, and the experience sparked my passion for VFX.
